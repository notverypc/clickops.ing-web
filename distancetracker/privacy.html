Distance Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Distance Tracker is an iPhone app that shows how far you have walked and run each week, month and year. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: your data stays on your device. The app does not collect, send or share anything.

What the app reads

With your permission, Distance Tracker reads walking and running workouts from Apple Health. For each workout it reads:

  • whether it was a walk or a run
  • when it started and ended
  • the distance covered
  • the name of the app or device that recorded it

The app asks for read access only. It never writes to Apple Health, and it does not read any other kind of health data.

How that information is used

The workouts are used for one purpose: to work out and display your distance totals, trends and comparisons inside the app. All of this happens on your iPhone.

What is stored

Distance Tracker does not keep its own copy of your workouts. It reads them from Apple Health each time it is opened. The only things it saves are your settings, such as your distance unit and the day your week starts on. These are stored on your device.

What is not done

  • No health data leaves your device. The app has no server and no accounts.
  • No health data is stored in iCloud by the app.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising or marketing.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software.

Your choices

You can change or withdraw the app's access to your workouts at any time in the Settings app, under Health › Data Access & Devices › Distance Tracker. Deleting the app removes its saved settings. Your workouts in Apple Health are not affected.

Children

Distance Tracker is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts to handle your data differently, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
