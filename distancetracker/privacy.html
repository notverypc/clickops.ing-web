D!stance Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

D!stance is an iPhone app that shows how far you have walked and run each week, month and year, and how many steps you take each day. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short: your data stays on your device. The app does not collect, send or share anything.

What the app reads

With your permission, D!stance reads two kinds of data from Apple Health.

Walking and running workouts. For each workout it reads:

  • whether it was a walk or a run
  • when it started and ended
  • how long you were moving, not counting any pauses
  • the distance covered
  • the name of the app or device that recorded it

Step count. It reads the number of steps you have taken, as totalled by Apple Health from your iPhone and Apple Watch. Steps are kept separate from your workouts and never count towards your distance.

The app asks for read access only. It never writes to Apple Health, and it does not read any other kind of health data.

How that information is used

Your workouts and steps are used for one purpose: to work out and display your distance totals, moving time, step counts, progress towards your daily step target, trends and comparisons, in the app and its Home Screen widgets. All of this happens on your iPhone.

What is stored

D!stance reads your data from Apple Health each time it is opened or a widget updates. Apple Health can't be read while your iPhone is locked, so the app keeps a small copy for its widgets to use until you next unlock it:

  • your walking and running workouts from the start of last year onwards
  • today's step count, and the day it belongs to

This copy is stored only on your iPhone, in a space shared by the app and its widgets. It is replaced each time the app or a widget reads Apple Health, and it is excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

The app also saves your settings, such as your distance unit, the day your week starts on and your daily step target. These are stored on your device.

What is not done

  • No health data leaves your device. The app has no server and no accounts.
  • No health data is stored in iCloud by the app.
  • No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
  • No data is used for advertising or marketing.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software.

Your choices

You can change or withdraw the app's access to your workouts and steps at any time in the Settings app, under Health › Data Access & Devices › D!stance. Deleting the app removes its saved copy and its settings. Your data in Apple Health is not affected.

Children

D!stance is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts to handle your data differently, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
