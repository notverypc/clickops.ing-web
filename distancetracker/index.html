D!stance

Your walking and running distances, compared with last week, month and year.

D!stance is a simple iPhone app that shows your walking and running distances. You can see how far you've gone this week, this month and this year, and how that compares with the week, month and year before.

Widgets put those comparisons on your Home Screen, so you can check how you're doing without opening the app.

Your data stays with you

D!stance only reads what is already saved in Apple Health. It never writes to Apple Health, and nothing leaves your iPhone. There are no accounts, no tracking and no ads.
